Important Details

General Information

Policies, workflow, and terms for all commission requests.

Pricing

  • Base pricing includes one character only
  • Additional characters, props, and backgrounds cost extra depending on complexity
  • Complex character designs may incur additional fees
  • Prices do not include 5% sales tax

Revisions

  • 1 major revision during sketch phase
  • 2 minor revisions during coloring phase
  • Additional revisions may incur extra charges
  • Major revision examples: pose changes, expression changes

Communication

If you have questions, updates, or simply want to chat about your commission, contact me through VGen or email. I normally send a follow-up email after one day if there is no reply on messaging platforms.

Commercial Usage

Tier 1 (+50%)

  • Twitch screens
  • YouTube thumbnails
  • Personal websites
  • Convention handouts

Tier 2 (+200%)

  • Merchandise
  • Digital sales
  • Reproduction/resale

Workflow

  1. Request discussion and quotation
  2. Sketch and color draft approval
  3. Final illustration process
  4. Finished artwork delivered via email

Payment

  • Full payment upfront
  • Work begins only after payment is received

Terms of Service

Usage

  • Please use commissions for your own exclusive personal use
  • Do not use commissions for commercial use or distribution unless discussed

Intellectual Property

  • Some commissioned works may be used in my portfolio. If you want your piece to remain private, please mention it during discussion.
  • Extensive external modifications (painting over or AI modifications) are not allowed without permission.
  • You may not use my artwork for AI training.
  • My artworks are not authorized for blockchain-related technologies such as NFTs or cryptocurrency.

Refunds

  • No refunds

Conduct

  • Harassment, spam, or repeated rule violations may result in blacklist/rejection
  • I reserve the right to reject any commission request
