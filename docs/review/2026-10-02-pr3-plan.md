## How the pieces connect

**Ids:** each `FormFieldGroup`, `CheckboxField` and `RadioGroup` makes its own unique ids: one for the control, one for
its error list. A small shared counter keeps them unique across the page.

**Text fields (`FormFieldGroup` + `appControl`):**

```html
<div class="text-sm font-semibold">
  <p class="flex gap-2">
    <label [for]="controlId">{{ label }}</label>
    <app-required-marker />
    <app-field-error-list [id]="errorId" />
  </p>
  <ng-content />
  <!-- the <input appControl> -->
</div>
```

- `FormFieldGroup` provides its ids to its projected control.
- `Control` injects them, optionally, and sets:
  - `[attr.id]`;
  - `[attr.aria-invalid]`, which is `"true"` or absent;
  - `[attr.aria-describedby]`, the error list's id while errors show.
- Without a `FormFieldGroup` (none today), `Control` sets none of these.

**Checkbox (`CheckboxField`):** the `<label>` keeps the input and its text together, as now. The error list moves out
of the label row, as it already is, and gets an id. The input gets `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby` the same way.

**Radio group (`RadioGroup`):** a `<fieldset>` with `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`, and the label becomes its
`<legend>`, still followed by the `[labelExtra]` "?" button. The error list keeps its place under the group.

**Field errors (`FieldErrorList`):** takes an `id` input and puts it on its wrapper. The wrapper is always rendered, empty
when there's no error, so `aria-describedby` never points at a missing element.

**First invalid field (decision 2):** `createFormSubmission`'s `onInvalid` gets the form. After setting the status, it
calls `form().errorSummary()[0]?.fieldTree().focusBoundControl()`. Signal Forms lists the errors in field order, and
each field can focus its own bound input.

**Pricing pick (decision 5):** `RequestForm` gains `focusCommissionType()`, which focuses the checked radio with
`preventScroll: true`. That way the smooth scroll to the form still runs, and focus is already there when it ends.
`Commission.onPick` calls it after `selectCategory`.

**Language toggle:** a new key, `components.language_toggle.switch` ("switch language" / "言語を切り替え"). The
button's `aria-label` is `"{label}, {switch}"`. The old `toggle` key goes.

## Risks and assumptions

- **No screen reader in tests:** what's announced is checked through ARIA attributes and axe, not by listening. The
  owner can try VoiceOver (⌘F5 on a Mac) on the forms.
- **`focusBoundControl` on a radio group:** with several radios bound to one field, Signal Forms may focus the first
  radio rather than the checked one. If so, `focusCommissionType()` focuses the checked radio directly, and the
  first-invalid-field case is unaffected (an empty radio group can't happen, since one is always chosen).
- **axe in jsdom:** it checks structure, names and ARIA, but not contrast or anything that needs layout.
- **`<legend>` styling:** browsers give legends special layout. It's reset to a plain block, so the label row looks the
  same.
